# Product Sorting Test - Price Low to High

## Website
SauceDemo

## Feature Tested
Product sorting by price from low to high

## Test Steps

1. Logged into SauceDemo using the standard test account.
2. Opened the products page.
3. Selected "Price (low to high)" from the sorting dropdown.
4. Checked the prices of the products from top to bottom.

## Expected Result

Products should be displayed from the lowest price to the highest price.

## Actual Result

The products were correctly sorted from the lowest price to the highest price.
